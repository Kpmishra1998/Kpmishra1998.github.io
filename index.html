Kaibalyapati Mishra

Assistant Professor of Economics
Christ University, Bannerghatta Road Campus, Bengaluru

I am an applied economist working on the economics of education, labour markets and health in India. My research studies how asymmetric information, screening and signalling shape access to higher education, the value of degrees in the labour market, and the inequalities that follow from both.

I completed my Ph.D. at the Centre for Economic Studies and Policy (CESP), Institute for Social and Economic Change (ISEC), Bengaluru, under the guidance of Prof. Krishna Raj. My thesis is titled “Asymmetric Information in Higher Education in India: A Study of Screening, Signaling, and Inequality in Access.” Before joining Christ University, I was a Senior Research Fellow (Health Economist) at the Centre of Excellence on Public Health and Nutrition (CoE-PHN), National Institute of Technology Rourkela.

I also write regularly on education, labour and public policy for The Hindu and Deccan Herald.
