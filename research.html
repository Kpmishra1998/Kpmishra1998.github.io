  2. Private School Choice in India: Disparities in Enrollment and Experience. Journal of School Choice, 2026. Featured in the IHDS Research Digest, June 2026.
    Abstract

    This study examines the factors behind households’ preference for private education and quantifies inter- and intra-group disparities in private school attainment in India. Using the India Human Development Survey II for children aged 8 to 11, it constructs psychometric indicators of school experience and perception through confirmatory factor analysis and applies Fairlie decomposition across gender, region and caste. Positive school experience and negative school perception are both correlated with private school attendance. The rural-urban disparity (33%) is larger than the gender disparity (9.6%), and Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes face gaps of 20.5% and 26.5% relative to other castes. Household wealth and income are the main drivers of these gaps, and interaction analysis suggests that raising asset endowments could narrow regional and gender disparities.
